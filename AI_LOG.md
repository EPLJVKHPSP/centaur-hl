## AI-assisted work

AI tools inspected the source files and API documentation, wrote the calculation and dashboard, researched product labels and retrieved a cached 204-wallet API sample. A separate API pass paginated the 24 initially capped wallets; the final cache supplies 196 usable comparisons, five inconsistencies and three empty histories. No full-population coverage is claimed.

The 2 November review compared the implementation with the full brief. It corrected the profitable-volume denominator, volume-decile grouping, zero-result sign-change count, fomo's type and unsupported or overconfident tag evidence. It shortened the findings and documentation, repaired mobile chart sizing and rebuilt the submission.

After the user's repository inspection and cleanup decisions, AI consolidated historical form-factor research into `research_evidence/`, removed redundant submission files, updated documentation and rebuilt the 16-file archive. The user directed the cleanup and reviewed its scope; AI performed the filesystem operations and verification.

On 2 November the user requested an AI-assisted review of product tags for all 30 companies. AI-assisted research identified candidate tags from first-party product sites and feature documentation. The recorded claims and inclusion rules were then reviewed, with manual judgment used for ambiguous classifications and unsupported tags removed. All ten permitted tags were assessed for each company (300 recorded decisions); the verified positive tags, URLs, short evidence and confidence were recorded in the `make_labels.py` research seed. Each `labels.csv` row also records all ten decision statuses, distinguishing features not found in reviewed sources from those with insufficient information. Because the assignment asks for **“any product tags”**, a company can legitimately have none: six companies were deliberately left with empty product-tag cells rather than assigned speculative tags. The script replays the reviewed evidence deterministically and makes no AI calls when rebuilding. The user's four targeted manual judgments above do not imply hands-on verification of every feature; uncertain claims remain available for further checking.

## Checks actually performed

All eight required metrics were independently recomputed from raw records for all 122 companies. Synthetic tests cover zero/missing PnL, grouping, exclusions, source labels, pagination boundaries, timezones and decile membership. Browser checks exercised filtering, search, sorting, details, signed axes and offline operation at laptop and two phone widths. The findings were printed to A4, checked for one page and visually inspected. Source reachability and targeted feature-content checks were performed separately; a reachable link alone was not treated as proof.

These are AI-executed checks, alongside the targeted human inspection and judgments described above; they are not a claim of independently performed end-to-end human QA or funded-trade testing.
