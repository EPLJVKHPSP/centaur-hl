How to read this data

Definitions and limits of the source data.

01 / Source and identity

Public refCode and strictly > $1m 30d leaderboard volume. Builders sharing the same normalized X account ID are one company. Leaderboard totals remain authoritative for volume and fees; wallet addresses are deduplicated inside each company. The same wallet in two distinct companies is a separate observation in both, and full-account PnL can repeat.

02 / Attribution and robustness

Routed volume and fees are builder-specific. Wallet perpPnl may include activity through other builders and direct routing. Shares within selected builders exclude direct and other-builder volume. Largest-one / largest-ten wallet exclusions test sensitivity; their denominator subtracts measured routed volume from the leaderboard figure approximately. The separate historical-fill audit tests selected-builder share against a wallet's available Hyperliquid fill volume for a limited sample.
